# Research Methodology Standards

Apply these rules to all R&D research outputs (scouting, literature review, feasibility, reports).

## Evidence and citations

- Every major claim must cite at least one source with title, date, and link
- High-impact claims require two independent sources
- Label single-source or opinion-based statements as **low confidence**
- Distinguish facts, assumptions, and recommendations explicitly

## Reproducibility

- Document search queries, databases, and date of search
- Record methodology steps so another analyst could repeat the work
- Version research documents (1.0, 1.1, …)

## Objectivity

- Present counter-evidence and limitations
- Avoid vendor bias — disclose sponsorship or preferred vendors
- Score alternatives with documented criteria before recommending

## Output quality

- Executive summary ≤ 200 words
- Use tables for comparisons
- End with actionable next steps and owner suggestions

## Handoff

- Research that recommends action must link to **research-to-requirements** or **feasibility-study** recommendation section
- Archive final reports under `doc/research/`
