# Research Ethics and Data Handling

Apply to all R&D work involving data collection, human subjects, or sensitive information.

## Data handling

- Do not include secrets, credentials, or PII in research reports or experiment logs
- Anonymize datasets used in examples
- Follow organizational data classification policies

## Reproducibility and honesty

- Report negative results and failed experiments — do not cherry-pick
- Document known limitations and confounders
- Do not fabricate citations or experiment data

## Human subjects

- If research involves users or personal data, confirm IRB or privacy approval before proceeding
- When in doubt, escalate to compliance

## IP respect

- Do not reproduce copyrighted material beyond fair-use excerpts with citation
- Respect third-party license terms in spike code
- Prior-art search outputs are awareness only — not legal clearance

## Safety

- Do not design experiments that could harm systems, data, or people without explicit approval
- Prototype spikes must not run against production environments without authorization
