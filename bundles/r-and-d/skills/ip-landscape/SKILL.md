# IP Landscape

Build a strategic view of patent activity in a technology domain.

## Disclaimer

R&D awareness only — **not legal advice**. Formal freedom-to-operate requires qualified IP counsel.

## When to Use

- Entering a new technology market
- Long-horizon R&D roadmap planning
- After prior-art-search for a broader view

## Procedure

### Step 1: Define the technology area

- Scope boundary (what is in / out)
- Key technical terms and classifications
- Major competitors and assignees to watch

### Step 2: Collect patent clusters

Group patents by:
- Assignee (who owns the space)
- Sub-technology (e.g., sensing, communication, UI)
- Filing trend (growing / stable / declining)

### Step 3: Identify white space

Areas with:
- Few filings but high technical relevance
- Expiring patents opening design freedom
- Geographic gaps

| | | | |

### Step 5: Strategic recommendations

- Where to invest R&D
- Where to license or partner
- Where to avoid or design around
