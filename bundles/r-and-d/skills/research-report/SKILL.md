# Research Report

Produce a consistent, stakeholder-ready research report.

## When to Use

- Closing a research or scouting phase
- Presenting findings to architecture or product owners
- Archiving R&D decisions for traceability

## Report structure

1. **Executive summary** (≤ 200 words)
2. **Background and motivation**
3. **Methodology** (how research was conducted)
4. **Findings** (evidence-based, grouped by theme)
5. **Analysis and discussion**
6. **Recommendations** (actionable, prioritized)
7. **Risks and open questions**
8. **References and appendices**

Use `references/report-template.md`. Follow **research-methodology** rule for citations.

## Quality bar

- Every recommendation links to supporting evidence
- Distinguish facts from assumptions
- Include date and author
- State confidence level for major conclusions
